# STOCK Act & congressional disclosures (reference)

Educational summary for `persona-pelosi`. Not legal advice.

## STOCK Act basics (2012)

- Members of Congress, senior staff, and the President must disclose securities transactions.
- **PTR** (Periodic Transaction Report) required within **30 days of notification**, **no later than 45 days** after the trade.
- Threshold: transactions over **$1,000** in stocks, bonds, commodities futures (ranges reported, not exact amounts).
- **Prohibits** trading on material nonpublic information obtained through official duties.

Annual financial disclosure forms (covering broader holdings) are separate from PTRs.

## PTR vs SEC Form 4

| | Congressional PTR | SEC Form 4 (corporate insiders) |
|--|-------------------|----------------------------------|
| Lag | Up to 45 days | ~2 business days |
| Detail | Dollar ranges | Exact shares and price |
| Enforcement | Weak ($200 late fee common) | SEC civil/criminal |

## Copy-trade caveats

1. **Lag** — 30–50 days means entry may be far from the member's.
2. **Ranges** — "$500,001–$1,000,000" is not a precise size signal.
3. **Spouse trades** — Often filed under member name; may not reflect member's decision.
4. **Options exercises** — Deep ITM LEAP exercises look like huge buys; economics differ from open-market purchase.
5. **No causation proof** — Outperformance of disclosed portfolios ≠ illegal edge; survivorship and reporting bias apply.

## Policy research (legal)

- **Congress.gov** — bill text, status, committees
- **Federal Register** — rules and proposed rules
- Agency press releases and **public** hearing schedules

Do not use leaked bill text or private markup as trade triggers.
