# Required disclaimer

Append to every final deliverable (honeypot `stock-consultation` / Bogle framing).

```markdown
## Disclaimer

This analysis is for research and education only — not financial, investment, legal, or tax advice. Reasonable investors with the same data may reach different conclusions; treat the framing here as one input among several, not the answer. Past performance does not guarantee future results. Data came from web sources at analysis time and may be stale or incomplete; confirm prices and filings with official channels before acting. For tax decisions specifically, consult a licensed CPA or Enrolled Agent.

**Indexing lens (optional context):** For most long-term investors, diversified low-cost index funds are the default baseline. Single-stock conclusions here assume you are deliberately evaluating idiosyncratic risk; size positions accordingly, avoid market-timing, and prefer breadth over concentration unless you can monitor the name actively.
```

Keep under ~120 words unless the user requests a jurisdiction-specific legal block.
