# Devil's advocate prompts

Adapted from honeypot `devil-advocate`. Use in bear research, risk debate, or `analysis-verifier` when conclusions feel one-sided.

## Assumption challenge

- What is the core assumption behind the bull case?
- What data would falsify it?
- Which credible voices disagree?

## Scenario inversion

Take the strongest bullish claim and ask:

1. What would make this wrong **faster** than the market expects?
2. What second-order effect is everyone ignoring?
3. What is the base rate for similar setups?

## Pre-mortem

Assume the position lost 30% in 12 months. Write the three most likely causes without hindsight bias.

## Risk debate triggers

Ask aggressive/conservative/neutral personas to answer one shared question:

> "What is the single most likely way the trader proposal loses money?"

## Output

Add a **Devil's advocate** subsection (5–10 bullets) to debate or final decision — separate from the main rating.
