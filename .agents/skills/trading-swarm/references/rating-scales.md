# Rating scales

Use these labels exactly as written in final outputs.

## Portfolio rating (Research Manager, Portfolio Manager)

Pick **one**:

| Rating | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Buy** | Strong conviction; enter or add materially |
| **Overweight** | Constructive; increase exposure gradually |
| **Hold** | Evidence balanced; maintain current exposure |
| **Underweight** | Cautious; trim exposure |
| **Sell** | Strong bear conviction; exit or avoid |

Reserve **Hold** only when bull and bear cases are genuinely balanced. Otherwise commit to a side.

## Trader action (Trader only)

Pick **one**: **Buy** | **Hold** | **Sell**

The Trader translates the Research Manager plan into a concrete desk proposal. Nuanced Overweight/Underweight sizing belongs in the Research Manager and Portfolio Manager layers.
