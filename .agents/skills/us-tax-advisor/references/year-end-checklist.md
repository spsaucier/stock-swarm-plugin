# Year-end tax checklist

Use when a user asks about year-end planning, October–December timing, or "what should I do before December 31".

## Inputs to gather

- Realized gains/losses YTD (taxable accounts only)
- Unrealized P&L per lot (specific-lot detail if broker provides it)
- Estimated marginal bracket and filing status
- Any planned big income events (bonus, RSU vest, business sale)
- IRA / HSA / 401(k) contribution status

## Walk through in this order

### 1. Net realized gains/losses YTD

Pull from broker. Net short-term and long-term separately — they offset each other but the order matters for what's left exposed.

### 2. Harvest losses (taxable accounts)

For each unrealized loss position, ask:

- Is it a position you'd buy today? If no, harvest is a free lunch (still need wash-sale-safe replacement or 31-day wait)
- Will harvesting trigger a wash sale (purchase within 30 days before or 30 days after, including in IRA / spouse's account)?
- Is the loss short-term or long-term? Short-term losses are more valuable when offsetting short-term gains

Cap on net loss against ordinary income: **$3,000/year** ($1,500 MFS); excess carries forward indefinitely.

### 3. Defer or accelerate gains

- Position about to cross 1-year mark? **Wait** — short-term to long-term is usually a 10–20% rate difference
- Big gain you'd take next year anyway? **Accelerate or defer** based on which year has lower expected income

### 4. Roth conversion window

Only relevant if user mentions retirement accounts. Convert traditional IRA dollars to Roth in **low-income years**; pay tax now at a lower rate to grow tax-free. Must complete by **December 31** for that tax year (no IRA-style April 15 lookback).

### 5. Charitable giving — appreciated shares

If the user itemizes and is in a high bracket:

- Donate **long-term appreciated shares** directly to charity / DAF
- Avoid the cap gains tax entirely
- Get the deduction at fair market value
- Replace the position with cash if you still want it

### 6. Fund tax-advantaged accounts

- 401(k) — must come out of W-2 by year-end; can't be funded retroactively
- HSA — if HDHP-eligible all year
- IRA — has lookback to April 15 of next year; do not rush

### 7. State considerations

Flag the existence; don't solve. Common ones:

- States with no income tax (no harvesting urgency at state level)
- High-tax states (CA, NY, NJ, etc.) — state cap gains often taxed as ordinary income
- Move during the year — partial-year residency rules

### 8. Estimated payments

If user has been generating significant gains, surface the **safe harbor** rule (pay 100% of prior year tax, 110% if AGI > $150k) to avoid underpayment penalties. Don't compute the number — point to a CPA.

## What to put in the answer

Always present at least three columns:

1. The action
2. The estimated benefit (range, with caveats)
3. The conditions under which "do nothing" is the better call

## Pre-flight check before any action

- Wash sale risk across all accounts (including spouse, IRA, automatic reinvestment)
- Settlement date implications for late-December trades (T+1 settlement; trade date is usually what matters for tax, but verify)
- Pending corporate actions (mergers, splits)
- Cost basis method on file with broker (FIFO, LIFO, specific lot)

## When to escalate to a CPA immediately

- Trader tax status / §475(f) election — deadline-sensitive
- Roth conversion of large balances
- ESPP / RSU sales with grant-year complexity
- Multi-state moves
- AMT exposure
- NIIT thresholds in play
- Cryptocurrency with cross-chain or DeFi activity
