# Persona frameworks (quick reference)

Anchors for the five persona skills. Agents read the full `SKILL.md` per persona; this is a cheat sheet.

## persona-wsb — high-beta retail

- **Horizon:** days–8 weeks
- **Edge:** catalyst + social velocity + convexity
- **Risk:** 1–5% speculative sleeve; defined risk only
- **Fails when:** dilution, post-squeeze drift, illiquidity

## persona-buffett — quality value

- **Horizon:** 5–10+ years
- **Edge:** moat + owner earnings + margin of safety
- **Risk:** business impairment, not chart noise
- **Fails when:** value trap, overpaying for quality

## persona-oneil — CAN SLIM

- **Horizon:** weeks–months per trade
- **Edge:** C+A growth + L leader + base breakout + M market uptrend
- **Risk:** 7–8% stop, no averaging down
- **Fails when:** wrong market stage, extended chase, laggard RS

### Minervini 8-point trend template (overlay)

1. Price above 150-day and 200-day MA  
2. 150-day MA above 200-day MA  
3. 200-day MA trending up ≥ 1 month  
4. 50-day MA above 150/200  
5. Price above 50-day MA  
6. Price ≥ 30% above 52-week low  
7. Price within 25% of 52-week high  
8. RS rank in top quartile of universe  

## persona-burry — contrarian deep value

- **Horizon:** 2–5+ years
- **Edge:** margin of safety + forensic work + lonely consensus
- **Risk:** downside first; careful with shorts
- **Fails when:** value trap, illiquidity, right thesis wrong time

## persona-pelosi — policy & disclosure

- **Horizon:** weeks–quarters (tactical)
- **Edge:** public legislation map + lagged PTR patterns (not MNPI)
- **Risk:** stale filings, bill failure, narrative ≠ economics
- **Fails when:** chasing disclosed trades after move
